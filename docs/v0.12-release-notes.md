# Gesture IME v0.12.0 release notes

v0.12.0 is an ARM64 APK for Android 9 (API 28) and later. Input, conversion, emoji recents, and the bundled catalog stay on the device; the app has no network permission.

## Emoji catalog scrolling

- The emoji layer now uses its top three rows as one continuous vertical viewport: newest-first, distinct device-local recents (up to eight) continue directly into the bundled catalog. With no recents, the first 24 catalog emojis fill the viewport.
- The bottom row remains fixed for layer navigation and deletion. It does not move while the catalog scrolls.
- A vertical drag scrolls the viewport and cancels emoji tap, long-press, and flick completion. A normal tap commits once and refreshes recents. Pointer cancellation, capture loss, editor changes, and other interrupted gestures do not commit an emoji.

## Verification and known limits

- API 36 arm64 emulator evidence covers the empty-recent viewport, catalog scrolling, direct emoji commit, recent refresh, fixed controls, and phone/wide Light/Dark layouts. Focused Robolectric tests and the full fast-check, Android unit, lint, and APK suite passed during release preparation.
- The physical Fold device and on-device Japanese speech recognition remain to be verified. The APK is arm64-v8a only.
